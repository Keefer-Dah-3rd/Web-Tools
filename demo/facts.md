# Lumen (demo product)

A desk lamp with a dial instead of an app. Turn it for brightness, push it for warm or cool.
- Made by a two-person studio in Leeds, 2025.
- 1,800 lumens at full brightness; 4 W at the lowest setting.
- 41 prototypes before the dial felt right; the first 30 used a slider.
- Ships with no account, no Wi-Fi and no firmware updates.
